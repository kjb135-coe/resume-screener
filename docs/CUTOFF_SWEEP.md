## Caveat

These cutoffs are chosen on the same 60 resumes they are scored against, so the gain is an upper bound rather than a held-out result. With 20 per class there is no honest train/test split available. Treat a win here as a reason to re-run the full eval with the new cutoffs, not as a measured improvement on its own -- and note that PLAN.md section 3c measured 10% verdict drift between identical runs, which is larger than several of the differences in these tables.
